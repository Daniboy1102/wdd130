Iron Rapids Co.

Our mission is to deliver unforgettable rafting adventures while promoting safety, teamwork, and respect for nature. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced rafter, our professional guides are here to help you conquer the rapids and create memories that last a lifetime.

History

Founded in 2005, Iron Rapids Co. began as a small group of adventure enthusiasts with a passion for rivers and the outdoors. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted rafting company known for our experienced guides, quality equipment, and commitment to adventure. From our humble beginnings with just two rafts and a dream, we've expanded to serve thousands of adventure seekers each year while maintaining our commitment to safety and environmental stewardship.
