Featured Adventures

Los Dientes del Diablo

Class V · Expert

The most challenging run in our fleet. "The Devil's Teeth" features relentless Class V rapids, sheer canyon walls, and no easy exits. Only for experienced paddlers with strong whitewater skills. An unforgettable bucket-list experience.

  • Duration: Full Day (8 hrs)
  • Min. Age: 18
  • Price: $175 / person
Book Now

Big Mallard

Class III · Intermediate

A perfect mix of scenic calm stretches and exciting rapids, the Big Mallard is our most popular trip for adventurous beginners and returning paddlers. Beautiful riverbanks, wildlife sightings, and a fun challenge await.

  • Duration: Half Day (4 hrs)
  • Min. Age: 12
  • Price: $95 / person
Book Now

Half Day Family Adventure

Class I–II · Beginner

Designed for families with young children or first-time rafters, this gentle float takes you through calm waters with small, fun riffles. Our guides keep things exciting and educational with nature commentary along the way.

  • Duration: Half Day (3 hrs)
  • Min. Age: 6
  • Price: $65 / person
